## Caveats that decide the conditions
1. **Inflow is uniform, not nested.** The solver's inflow BC takes one velocity vector, so per-cell boundary values from the 20 m field are not possible without changing XLB. Effect: far above the street the 5 m run is much faster than the 20 m field (median 0.83 vs 0.28 at 50 m), because the 20 m city run builds its boundary layer over 8 km of fetch and the 5 m tile only over its 400 m margin. Absolute speeds and the ratio to the 20 m field are therefore not meaningful; only the patterns (wakes, channelling, shelter) are. **Condition 1:** add a log-law or 20 m-derived inflow profile (needs a custom per-node inflow BC), or scale the 5 m field per height to the 20 m west-edge value, before use.
2. **No validation against observation or a published street-canyon benchmark.** The cube test passes on the solver (see config note), but cell Re is about 12 so wakes are longer than real. **Condition 2:** check against a station or canyon benchmark.
3. Boundary artefact: max speed ratio 6.0 sits on the east outlet face (cell 559, 284, 7); 93 cells above 2 exist, all at the box edge. Fluid median 1.06, 99.9th percentile 1.33. The 400 m margin keeps this away from the core, but a despike like the 20 m runs use is advisable. rho ranged 0.48 to 1.14 at those same cells.
