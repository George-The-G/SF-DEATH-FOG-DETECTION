Death Fog Viewer Redesign

Viewer redesign: cleaner chrome, more city

Design plan and mockups for the 3-D viewer's controls. Every screen is drawn at real phone size (390 × 844) on top of a real frame from the live preview, then scaled down. Route names and numbers are examples. Nothing here is built or deployed.

What goes away or merges

  • The expanding weather grid is gone. All values sit on one line, including rain; the source moves to the status line.
  • Three stacked bottom cards (weather, legend, time bar) become one dock with three rows.
  • The full-width legend shrinks to a 52 px ramp next to the weather values, labelled dry and icy fog.
  • The words "observed" and "forecast" over the bar are replaced by the bar itself: solid for observed hours, dotted for forecast, yellow tick for the data time. The clock's second line says which one you are on.
  • The big "Proof of concept" pill and the "Data as of" line merge into one quiet status line at the bottom. Tapping it still opens About.
  • The outer frame around the top row is dropped; the route chip and skate button float on their own.

What stays where it is

  • Skate routes up top: route chip, skate flyover button, and the flyover progress, pause and Exit while flying.
  • Fog and time at the bottom: fog play button, time bar, weather, legend, settings.
  • Route info under every route in the list: name, then length, drop and climb.
  • The existing skateboard and cloud play icons, unchanged.
  • Every live item already built: day labels, data time, delayed and forecast-only notices, route wetness, City and District street layers, Live (beta) night.

The one bold move

  • A thin wetness profile under the selected route chip and beside each route in the list: the route's streets, start to finish, coloured by the same ramp as the map. You see where the slick part is before you fly it.
