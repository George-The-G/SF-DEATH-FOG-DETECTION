What changed against today's bar

  • One split button replaces the play button's spot, same 60 px width. Top third: the four chevrons (speed). Bottom two thirds: play/pause, twice the height of the speed part. The whole button is 54 px tall, 10 px more than today's 44 px.
  • Legend and settings moved out of the bar: the dry/wet gradient is a tab on the bar's top edge, flush with its left side. The settings button sits left of the fog (cloud) button. On desktop, both stay anchored to the right of the bottom bar.
  • Bottom row removed: the BETA tag, date range and disclaimer text are gone. A small ⓘ icon sits at the right end of the weather row and opens the "5 min sim, not a forecast" note. The bar is 18 px shorter than today's (105 px against 123 px).
  • Alignment: the tab, weather text and track share one left edge; the clock, ⓘ and fog button share the right edge.
  • Clock: monospaced at a fixed width so the digits stay put. It redraws at most 10 times a second, and the colon blinks only at the two slowest speeds. Forecast shows in amber.
  • Settings sheet: the old 1x / 4x / 16x control is removed in beta.
  • Feedback: a short label names the setting on each tap, for example "Fast, 1 minute per second". Paused: the speed part dims but keeps its setting.
  • Watch on a phone: the speed part is about 18 px tall, small for a thumb. The tap area can extend into the gap above the button without changing how it looks, if you want that.
