# Station source check, guide 03 step 1

Checked 2026-11-12T22:36:42 UTC on 2026-11-12 (about 14:36 PST), from the user's PC, with real requests. Script and raw results were kept outside the repo; this file is the report. Nothing in the model was changed by this step.

Method: every request used the project User-Agent (`sf-death-fog (github.com/George-The-G/SF-DEATH-FOG-DETECTION)`), no account, no key. Pace: one request per 0.6 s or slower (about 160 requests in total, spread over roughly ten minutes); the script stops after 6 failures in a row (none happened; a few 404s mean 'this station has no such product').

Grid: EPSG:32610 x 540100-557200, y 4171500-4187720. 'km to grid' is the distance from the station to the nearest edge of that rectangle (0.0 = inside). 'age' is the minutes between the station's newest observation and the time of the check. A station counts as 'usable now' when it carries at least one of T, Td/RH, wind and its newest report is at most 90 minutes old. In the variable columns: Y = in at least 80 % of the 8-12 newest reports, part = in some, - = never seen.

## Notes that matter for the model

- **Heights are not in any feed.** NWS, AWC and IEM give the station ground elevation; CO-OPS and NDBC give none (IEM's HADS copy of the same station supplies one, shown above). Sensor heights above ground (anemometer 10 m at airports, different on piers and buoys) are on the station pages only. Needed for guide 03 step 3 if we want to correct to roof level.
- **GGBC1 (Golden Gate Bridge centre)**: IEM lists elevation -95 m, which is wrong (the deck is about 67 m above water); only the wind sensor reports (speed and direction), at an irregular 15-30 minute cadence. Treat position as exact, elevation as unknown.
- **SFOC1 (San Francisco downtown, HADS)** reports temperature, dew point and humidity hourly, no wind. The NWS `observations/latest` call returned empty values for it although the 12 newest reports in the list call carried them, so the list call is the one to use.
- **NWS copy of KSFO** carries wind in only 1 of 12 reports; the aviationweather.gov METAR feed for KSFO has all fields in all reports. Use AWC for airports. Visibility and ceiling come from METAR; the model already uses KHAF, KSFO and KOAK from AWC.
- **Visibility sensors on the bay**: CO-OPS 9414296 (Pier 17) and 9414797 (Oakland Berth 38) report visibility with air temperature and humidity every 6 minutes (also on NDBC as PXSC1 and OBXC1 with dew point and visibility in nautical miles). These two sit inside the grid and are the only direct fog-visibility readings in the city.
- **Wind on the bay side**: Pier 1 (PXOC1), Berth 34 (OKXC1), Oakland Middle Harbor (OMHC1), Berth 67 (LNDC1) and Alameda (AAMC1) all report wind every 6 minutes. They are sheltered waterfront sites east of the hills; Fort Point (FTPC1, already the model's wind reference) is the only west-side wind station in the grid besides the bridge.
- **Hills**: RAWS stations ONOC1 (Oakland North, 409 m), OKSC1 (Oakland South, 323 m), HSPC1 (Spring Valley, 314 m, 14 km south-west on the Peninsula crest), MDEC1 (Middle Peak, Marin, 587-713 m depending on the feed) report T, Td, wind. Their heights make them useful for the inversion height (above-fog temperature), not as street-level truth. MDEC1 is often above the fog top, which is why its dew point is near its temperature.
- **Age at the time of the check**: CO-OPS 6-minute stations 8-14 minutes; the same stations through NDBC text files 32-62 minutes (use CO-OPS for them); METARs 22-43 minutes (hourly, plus special reports); RAWS 19-64 minutes (hourly); hourly HADS stations 37-67 minutes; Golden Gate Bridge 67 minutes with an irregular cadence. KSQL (the only one with a long gap) had no report for almost 6 hours (it is not a 24 h station).
- Values are real observations of the afternoon of 2026-11-12 (a warm, clear afternoon: 19-22 C at the airports, 18.6 C on the bay piers); fog-time behaviour (for example how often visibility is reported when it is below 1 mile) was not tested by this check.

## Recommended list (for the user to confirm; nothing built)

Use as **hourly means of the newest reports**, one reading per station per hour, and drop a station for the hour when its newest report is older than 90 minutes.

**Temperature and dew point (spatial correction; need 4-8 well spread points)**
1. KSFO (south, 7.7 km), KOAK (east, 10.4 km), KHAF (coast south-west, 19.5 km) from AWC METAR: already used; keep. Add KHWD (20.6 km) only if more east-bay spread is wanted.
2. SFOC1 downtown (inside the grid, hourly T, Td, RH). Only in-city dew point; keep. Elevation 34-46 m.
3. FTPC1 / CO-OPS 9414290 Fort Point (inside, 6 min): air temperature, pressure, wind. Already used for wind and air temperature.
4. CO-OPS 9414311 Pier 1 and 9414296 Pier 17 (inside, 6 min): air temperature (Pier 17 also humidity and visibility). These are the bay-side cold/warm anchor and sit exactly where the fog edge arrives; recommended.
5. OAMC1 Oakland Museum and the NWS Oakland CWOP D3169 (T and Td, 10 minute cadence, 8.8 km): East Bay only; optional.
6. Do not use: D7094 and D6436 (empty), D3835 and D1132 (Orinda, hill, 17 km, sheltered), hill RAWS for street temperature (use them only for the inversion check).

**Wind (the weakest part today: one station)**
1. Keep FTPC1 / 9414290 Fort Point as the west-side reference (6 min).
2. Add GGBC1 (Golden Gate Bridge, inside the grid, up to 23 kt today; elevation is that of the deck, not the street) for the Gap flow and as a second west-side reference; cadence is irregular, so allow it to be missing.
3. Add the bay side: PXOC1 Pier 1, OKXC1 Berth 34, OMHC1 Middle Harbor (all inside the grid or within 2 km, wind every 6 minutes, same NOAA feed as FTPC1), and AAMC1 Alameda and LNDC1 Berth 67 further east. These show whether the wind that reached the city has turned or weakened on the lee side.
4. KSFO and KOAK METAR wind as the south and east references (already fetched). The hill RAWS (ONOC1, OKSC1, HSPC1) and MDEC1 are above the street layer; keep them out of the surface wind correction.
5. Offshore buoy 46026 (wind 8 m/s from 190 today) and 46237 San Francisco Bar (air and sea temperature) as upwind and sea references. They are outside the lattice, so they inform the fog source rather than a spatial correction.

**Humidity / fog**
1. Visibility: CO-OPS 9414296 Pier 17 and 9414797 Berth 38 (inside, 6 min) plus KSFO, KOAK, KHAF METAR visibility and ceiling. These are the only direct fog readings; recommended to be scored against the model's fog layer and used as a presence flag.
2. Dew point or RH: SFOC1, Pier 17 / Berth 38 (humidity and dew point), METARs.

**Sea temperature**
NDBC 46026 and 46237 (and 9414750 Alameda, bay side). One value is enough for the model's source term; buoys report every 10-30 minutes.

**Why this list**
- All are free, keyless, public-domain NOAA or NWS data (or the same data re-published), so there is no account to manage and licence terms allow re-use with attribution.
- It raises the wind references in or at the edge of the grid from 1 (Fort Point) to 6: three inside (FTPC1, GGBC1, PXOC1) and two within 2 km (OKXC1, OMHC1), plus the airports KSFO and KOAK a little further out. They cover the Gap, the east waterfront and the south and east airports. It does not cover the western neighbourhoods (Sunset, Richmond) or the hills: this check found no free keyless station there, so the spatial correction will be least certain west of Twin Peaks. SBMC1 (San Bruno Mountain) is inside the grid but currently carries no weather values.
- Every recommended station refreshes at least hourly and was under 70 minutes old when checked (KSQL, the only stale one at 350 minutes, is outside 20 km and not recommended).

**Needs the user's decision**
- Whether to include IEM (a university-run mirror, no SLA) for GGBC1 and the RAWS stations; the NWS station list did not return GGBC1, so there is no NOAA-direct keyless way to read it that this check found.
- Whether the hill and Marin RAWS should be used for the inversion check at all.
- Whether the in-grid pier stations should drive the spatial wind correction even though they are over water or on piers (wind ratio to roof level is unknown there).
